# Large writes land in sections

A document longer than a few screens, over three planned sections, or of a kind `handoff` § Where to write it names, lands per section under that section's mechanics (`~/.claude/skills/handoff/SKILL.md`); a truncated artifact is discarded, never shown as finished.

**In chat, pause at a clean breakpoint.** When a long response will not fit, write at full quality to the end of a section and close with `[PAUSED — X of Y complete. Send "continue" to resume from: <next section>]`. Skipping ahead to the conclusion is truncation chosen on purpose.
