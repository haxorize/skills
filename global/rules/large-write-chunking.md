# Large writes land in sections

Depends: `handoff`, `writing-for-humans`

A single oversized write — one tool call or chat message carrying a whole document or report — truncates silently, and a truncated artifact shown as finished reads as done and is not. A document over three planned sections, or of a kind that section names, lands per section under the mechanics `handoff` § Where to write it owns (`~/.claude/skills/handoff/SKILL.md`): resume pointer, marker-conflict stop, replace-not-append on resume, a truncated artifact discarded and never shown.

**In chat, pause at a clean breakpoint.** When a long response will not fit, write at full quality to the end of a section and close with `[PAUSED — X of Y complete. Send "continue" to resume from: <next section>]`. Skipping ahead to the conclusion is the same failure as truncation, chosen on purpose.
