## Decision

- **The round is closed on Nick's word of 2026-09-13** ("We can close the round after this", the sweep being "this"). Closed means: no batch is open, no review is owed, and the two mechanisms that read a running round — `feedback-loops`' close reading `Review cadence:` and `committing`'s fast path grepping `Deferrals register:` — are switched off by deleting the `## Round` block from `CLAUDE.md`, as that block's own last line instructs. Without it, a `Review required: yes` close names `/review-changes` and the register step does not run, which is the between-rounds behavior ADR-0080's 2026-09-04 family-4 amendment defines.
- **The register stays where it is and is not carried into the repo.** `~/code/lib/_rounds/2026-09-12/reconcile.md` § 4 (the deferrals register, 46 data rows at close, counted by `awk` over § 4's table) is the durable list of what this round set aside: the work-machine log carry (F87), the `[RISK]` lineage-slot wording (F61), the unasked `install.sh` run awaiting Nick's revert-or-keep word, the `audit-aeo` `Measured-tree:` carve-out, the five PENDING-ROUND transcript controls, the `frame-effort` wind tunnel's unattributed model, and the rest — each row naming where it lives and what unblocks it. The next round's briefing opens from that section, the way this round's opened from the 2026-09-04 register. Copying the open rows into an ADR or `CLAUDE.md` was rejected: the register is one file with one owner and a `Measured-tree:` line, and a second copy is the drift ADR-0081 through 0083 each recorded once already.
- **The ledger's park sweep is the next round's first read, not this record's.** `ledger.md` § Park sweep holds the re-parks with their unpark conditions; `D2.8` is marked unparked there at this close, and nothing else in it moved. The 2026-09-04 round's `BL-H31`/`G7` credential-hook control, carried through both rounds unread, stays a register row.

## Considered Options

- **A closing record per batch family instead of one per round.** Rejected: the families already have their admissions records, and what a close adds — the cadence departure, the register's location, the block deletion — is round-scoped.
- **No closing record, as the 2026-09-04 round had.** Rejected: ten admissions records point at a document that did not exist (ADR-0081–0084 and ADR-0086–0091), and the F24 departure would live only in a register row outside the repo.
- **Writing the 2026-09-04 closing record now, from this session.** Rejected as scope: that round's close was never called, its register in `../2026-09-04/reconcile.md` § Deferrals register is still read by this round's briefing, and its record is written by whoever calls it closed, with that round's numbers.
