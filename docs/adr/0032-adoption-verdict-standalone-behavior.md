# adoption-verdict as a standalone behavior, verdicts-only, machinery stripped

The 2026-07-19 Compound Engineering delta sweep found `ce-pov` materially evolved into a graded, project-grounded point-of-view engine — and confirmed a local gap: nothing in this suite renders an adoption verdict ("should we take X on?") as its own graded position; `grilling` extracts the *user's* decisions by asking, it never forms and defends the agent's own. We ported the method core as a new model-invoked behavior, `adoption-verdict`: the two-floor invalid-verdict gate (project floor + external floor, independent pass/fail, Hold subtypes on failure), the fixed grade vocabulary (Adopt/Trial/Hold/Reject/Not-our-problem), the verdict schema, and reversibility-tiered effort.

Three alternatives were rejected. Folding into `grilling` was rejected because verdict-rendering and question-asking are different acts with different failure modes — the same reasoning that reversed the earlier `diverging` fold-in. A user-invoked orchestrator was rejected because adoption questions arise mid-session and the model must reach the discipline unprompted. Porting CE's orchestration (scout fleets with dossier scratch-dirs, per-agent model tiers, the cross-model CLI peer panel) was rejected as harness-specific scale machinery, consistent with prior sweeps — grounding runs inline. One CE panel idea survived in local form: the correction-cost-gated second opinion became a fresh-context adversary subagent offered only on Tier 2/3 verdicts, landing the self-vs-fresh adversary fragment parked since the oaustegard sweep (fresh context beats self-critique because the author cannot unsee their own reasoning).
