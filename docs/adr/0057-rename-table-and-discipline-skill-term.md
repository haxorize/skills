Status: accepted (2026-08-22)

The 2026-08-21 mining round left naming decisions scattered across three records and a ledger: [ADR-0051](0051-usage-evidence-mining-round.md) decided `black-box-check` → `validate-behavior`, [ADR-0055](0055-first-domain-skill-phi-safe-code.md) and [ADR-0056](0056-first-off-path-skill-change-quiz.md) each deferred a working name, and the plan carried a glossary rename with no evidence either way. This record is the one place those were judged, as a table over all 44 skills in `src/` (`ls src | wc -l`) plus the 3 provisional names — 47 rows — on one criterion: a stranger reading `/` autocomplete — engineers and product or BA roles alike — recognises a user-invoked name, while a model-invoked name keeps trigger fidelity because no human types it. Ported skills keep their upstream name only where the port is near-direct and the name vendor-neutral (the ECC adaptation criterion, ledger A40.A21). Pairs-only naming in `DOMAIN.md` held: no Discipline skill lost its gerund and no Orchestrator gained one. Nick decided every row; the grill put the counter-case on each. This amends [ADR-0041](0041-black-box-check-ported-from-behavior-validator.md) and [ADR-0043](0043-wait-what-ported-name-kept.md), the records that chose the two names this table retires; each carries a dated pointer back.

## The glossary term

**Behavior skill** becomes **Discipline skill**, with `Behavior skill` kept as the listed alias. The plan's reason (`ledger.md` row D10) was that "behavior" collides with the product-behaviour sense, and `validate-behavior` makes the collision a skill name. The measurement that settled it, at `f4e6d56` with `grep -rIio '\b<word>\b' src/*/SKILL.md | wc -l`: skill bodies say "discipline" 31 times when describing what they hold (`grilling`: "the relentless-interview discipline"; `domain-modeling`: "the active discipline") and "behavior" 136 times, almost all meaning the thing under test. The term was the odd one out in the repo's own prose. The grill's first recommendation was to keep the term because the churn looked larger than the gain; on the count (`git grep -i -o 'behavior skill' | wc -l`) it is 20 occurrences in 5 files, four of them ADRs that stay as written, and churn is not a naming criterion.

## When names are judged

A name is judged at admission, at retirement, and when the skill's body gains or loses a mode — the `explain` case. It is not re-judged each mining round. This rule exists so "Names" does not recur as a batch that re-opens 44 rows every round; the must-NOT probe found nothing else forbidding it, and the port records it did miss (0041, 0043) are amended above.
