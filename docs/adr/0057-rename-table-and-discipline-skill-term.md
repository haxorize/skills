# One rename table over every skill name; the glossary term becomes Discipline skill

Status: accepted (2026-08-22)

The 2026-08-21 mining round left naming decisions scattered across three records and a ledger: [ADR-0051](0051-usage-evidence-mining-round.md) decided `black-box-check` → `validate-behavior`, [ADR-0055](0055-first-domain-skill-phi-safe-code.md) and [ADR-0056](0056-first-off-path-skill-change-quiz.md) each deferred a working name, and the plan carried a glossary rename with no evidence either way. This record is the one place those were judged, as a table over all 44 skills in `src/` (`ls src | wc -l`) plus the 3 provisional names — 47 rows — on one criterion: a stranger reading `/` autocomplete — engineers and product or BA roles alike — recognises a user-invoked name, while a model-invoked name keeps trigger fidelity because no human types it. Ported skills keep their upstream name only where the port is near-direct and the name vendor-neutral (the ECC adaptation criterion, ledger A40.A21). Pairs-only naming in `DOMAIN.md` held: no Discipline skill lost its gerund and no Orchestrator gained one. Nick decided every row; the grill put the counter-case on each. This amends [ADR-0041](0041-black-box-check-ported-from-behavior-validator.md) and [ADR-0043](0043-wait-what-ported-name-kept.md), the records that chose the two names this table retires; each carries a dated pointer back.

## The table

- **Renamed (3).** `black-box-check` → `validate-behavior`: ADR-0051's decision stands. The counter-case was `ledger.md` row D11's — the skill is on a retire-on-zero window and the rename spends edits on a name that may go — and it lost because the window should measure a name that says what the skill does; the retire check runs next window under the new name. `wait-what` → `explain`: the skill will gain a cold-topic mode (`/explain <topic>`, the surviving idea from Anthropic's `eli5` plugin; its picture-artifact form stays rejected, ledger A31.8), and `wait-what` presupposes a confusion the cold ask does not have. The rename lands now, names only, because `/explain` after a lost answer reads correctly against the body as it is; the mode itself is B5's body change, and A40.A21 stops protecting the port name once the body grows a mode. `change-quiz` → `merge-quiz`: the quiz is the gating half and "before merging" is the moment ADR-0056 names; `change-quiz` read as a quiz about change in the abstract. `catch-me-up` lost because it names the report half and hides the gate. The skill does not exist in `src/` yet, so this rename is recorded here and in ADR-0056 and lands with the B4c build.
- **Final as proposed (2).** `phi-safe-code` and `health-literacy` (the B10 skill, unnamed until this table). Both are model-invoked Domain skills, so the description carries the triggers and the name carries the domain noun. `plain-language` lost for `health-literacy` because it names no domain, and `member-copy` because it is one payer's word for the reader.
- **Kept (42).** Every other name in `src/`. Alternatives were tried for the rows that gave the stranger pause — `wizard` (`human-steps`, `manual-steps`), `harden-domain` (`refresh-glossary`), `address-findings` (`fix-findings`, `act-on-review`), `glapi-test-pass` (`glapi-gate`) — and each was a sideways move: `refresh-glossary` breaks the visible `domain-modeling` pairing, `fix-findings` overclaims since findings get declined, `wizard` is a near-direct neutral port. A rename the criterion does not demand is churn on typed muscle memory.
- **Not stubbed.** Neither of the two renamed skills in `src/` gets a deprecation stub: `grep -rl '"/<name>' ~/.claude/projects | wc -l` finds 0 transcript files for `/wait-what` and 0 for `/black-box-check` (13 for `/grill-and-record`, which does get one when the `grill-me` merge lands in B4c). The cost a rename cannot undo is typed muscle memory, and these two names had none.

## The glossary term

**Behavior skill** becomes **Discipline skill**, with `Behavior skill` kept as the listed alias. The plan's reason (`ledger.md` row D10) was that "behavior" collides with the product-behaviour sense, and `validate-behavior` makes the collision a skill name. The measurement that settled it, at `f4e6d56` with `grep -rIio '\b<word>\b' src/*/SKILL.md | wc -l`: skill bodies say "discipline" 31 times when describing what they hold (`grilling`: "the relentless-interview discipline"; `domain-modeling`: "the active discipline") and "behavior" 136 times, almost all meaning the thing under test. The term was the odd one out in the repo's own prose. The grill's first recommendation was to keep the term because the churn looked larger than the gain; on the count (`git grep -i -o 'behavior skill' | wc -l`) it is 20 occurrences in 5 files, four of them ADRs that stay as written, and churn is not a naming criterion.

## When names are judged

A name is judged at admission, at retirement, and when the skill's body gains or loses a mode — the `explain` case. It is not re-judged each mining round. This rule exists so "Names" does not recur as a batch that re-opens 44 rows every round; the must-NOT probe found nothing else forbidding it, and the port records it did miss (0041, 0043) are amended above.

## Deferred

- The cold-topic mode of `explain` and the re-pitch's survival inside it: B5 adds the mode as a branch, with the bare `/explain` staying the re-pitch, so the tighter discipline is the default and not the thing a topic argument replaces.
- The `grill-me` merge and the `grill-and-record` one-window stub: B4c, as a body change outside this record's scope.
- The bare shorthand "the `X` behavior" for a Discipline skill (37 lines in 28 files at review time, `grep -rIn 'the \`[a-z/-]*\` behavior\b' src DOMAIN.md README.md CLAUDE.md`, plus `write-skill`'s "Behavior vs orchestrator" heading): banned in the alias column now, swept in B4c as its first fold — 28 files is past what a names-only change carries.
- The `validate-behavior` retire check: next window, fire count under the new name.
