# The 2026-09-04 round's batch family 4g–4k admits material from twenty-six outside sources, all of it as local prose

## Context

Batches 4g–4k of the 2026-09-04 tightening round landed on 2026-09-04 as five commits — `1b4054b` (4g), `f51950e` (4h), `a8b13ee` (4i), `3494a73` (4j), `3c65adf` (4k) — folding rows from `~/code/lib/_rounds/2026-09-04/ledger.md` into one new reference (`src/writing-for-humans/references/recap-from-transcript.md`), one new section (`src/prototype/references/ui.md` § Defaults to refuse), and clauses across thirty-one other files under `src/`, plus `.claude/skills/mine-skills/SKILL.md`, `DOMAIN.md`, and `README.md` — 36 paths in all (`git diff --name-only 1b4054b~1 3c65adf | sort -u | wc -l` → 36). It also carries two figures the review re-measured and found wrong in the commit bodies (F26, F27); the bodies are history and stay as written, and the corrected figures live here and in the round's register.

## Considered Options

- **Leave the record to the round's closing ADR.** Rejected a third time, for the reasons ADR-0081 gave and ADR-0082 demonstrated: the closing ADR's date is still not fixed, five commits landed against an unwritten record, and an Apache-2.0 adaptation sat without attribution for the interval — the exact case the previous record named as its aggravator, repeated.
- **One record per batch.** Rejected as ADR-0082 rejected it: two upstreams span batches here — BMAD-METHOD across 4g and 4h, and affaan-m/ecc twice within 4k beside its ADR-0082 row — and the source is the unit that matches the lineage table, so the family is the smallest unit that holds each source once.
- **Rewrite the `ui.md` clusters independently of `anthropics/skills`** rather than attribute, the other disposition F20 offered. Rejected: the value of the passage is that it names the tells in the form a reader will meet them — the middle-dot string, the spaced em dash, the hex values — and a paraphrase that avoided those forms would stop naming the thing it exists to refuse. Attribution costs one row and one bullet; the rewrite costs the rule.
- **An attribution block inside `ui.md` and `register-by-artifact.md`** for the two Apache-2.0 sources. Rejected as ADR-0082 rejected it: neither upstream has a `NOTICE` file, so §4(d) asks for nothing to be reproduced; a skill body naming a repo record is what `lint-skills.sh` fails; and `CLAUDE.md` sends a reader to `docs/lineage.md` for lineage, which is where every other upstream's attribution lives.

## Consequences

- `docs/lineage.md`'s second table gains rows whose `Record` cell is this record — one per source, or per group of sources feeding one local file — and its header sentence names this record beside ADR-0082 as the case of a skill holding rows across both tables or across several second-table records.
- The round's closing ADR points here for batches 4g–4k rather than restating the lineage. The commit bodies of `3c65adf` and `1b4054b` keep their figures; the corrected ones are here and in the register, with the register naming this record as the correction's home.
