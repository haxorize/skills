# Chart-course keeps research tickets against upstream's inline-only redesign

Upstream wayfinder (mattpocock/skills, unmerged `wayfinder/research-inline` branch, 2026-07-13) removed research as a ticket type entirely — research runs inline as a subagent the moment a gap appears, findings captured on a throwaway `research/<name>` branch — arguing a research ticket buys "a session boundary bought for nothing." `chart-course` adopts only the mid-resolution half: a knowledge gap surfacing while resolving a ticket gets an inline research subagent, never a new research ticket. Charting-time research tickets stay, because our port never paid the cost upstream is escaping. They already fire in parallel at charting, so no session boundary is wasted. Resolution comments keep findings on the tracker where a team can see them (throwaway branches fit upstream's git-centric solo flow, and were already rejected once in the original port). And the type anchors the team-facing "research and prototype tickets are spikes" vocabulary bridge. The same branch audit adopted upstream's claims recheck (`wayfinder-recheck-claims-before-handoff`) unmodified, as an end-of-session pass covering both modes.
