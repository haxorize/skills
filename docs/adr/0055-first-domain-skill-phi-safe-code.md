# First Domain skill admitted: `phi-safe-code`, in plain `src/`

Status: accepted (2026-08-22)

The suite admits its first **Domain skill**, `phi-safe-code`: the discipline of keeping protected health information out of logs, fixtures, error messages, URLs, analytics events, and model prompts, with secure application logging as a section of it rather than a skill of its own. Three independent mining reads on 2026-08-22 (the everything-claude-code family, the security packs, and the awesome-list inventory) scored it against the Gap-and-stakes test and all three prongs held: the model's default code leaks identifiers into those surfaces unless told otherwise, the failure is a reportable breach, and every team that touches member data would load it. No source pack supplies the skill; the packs are clinic-shaped (EMR, ICD-10, Supabase RLS) and are mined for seed rules only. The name is final, judged with every other name in [ADR-0057](0057-rename-table-and-discipline-skill-term.md)'s rename table.

The alternative was a separate Humana-only repo holding every Domain skill, keeping this suite purely process-shaped as its `CLAUDE.md` describes it. A rational team would choose that, and it lost on one point: the Gap-and-stakes test admits only gaps that generalize past one employer (any payer, any regulated organization), and a second repo with one skill in it would not be installed. The org-specific layer, the allowed log-field list and retention figures, stays outside the suite as a project-conventions skill. The generalize-past-one-employer test does not bar the org-bound skills [ADR-0106](0106-admit-the-cvit-skills-as-org-bound.md) admits, `inventory-cvits` and `triage-cvits`.

Placement is plain `src/<name>/`, flat beside the process skills, with no new frontmatter and no new tier: every lint loop globs `src/*/SKILL.md`, and a nested tier would cost lint, install, and selftest changes for no reader. [ADR-0060](0060-third-domain-skill-accessible-ui.md) re-decided this at the third admission and kept it flat. Install scope is a separate question, decided in [ADR-0114](0114-opt-in-skills-install-per-repo-from-a-shared-skills-line.md): `phi-safe-code` is an opt-in skill, installed only into a repo whose `CLAUDE.md` lists it on a `Shared skills:` line, never globally. ADR-0060 also fixed that prong (a) measures the default output of the models the invoking teams run, not the authoring session's model.

The description fires on payer vocabulary plus the act, never only on "PHI" or "HIPAA". Six fresh-context probes, each given only the roster of model-invoked descriptions and one realistic message, measured this on 2026-08-22. "Add logging to the 834 loader so we can see why enrollments are getting dropped" and a request for seed data from a QA export loaded the skill with the word PHI in neither prompt; a billing-PDF probe stayed quiet. An NPI provider-directory rename over-fired, so the description gained an anti-trigger for provider-side data, which is public: an NPI is an NPPES identifier, and this body governs member and patient data. Against the corrected wording, an 837 parser-logging prompt fired and an NPI endpoint rename did not, quoting the anti-trigger back. The anti-trigger now reads "Not provider-side data (NPI, facility records; public)"; the dropped "provider directory" was re-probed on 2026-09-01 (manifest row `ph6`, `~/code/lib/_rounds/2026-08-29/trigger-test/`) and passed 2 of 2 with no `Skill` call. Those 2 reps ran on `claude-opus-5` under Claude Code 2.1.258, not the `claude-fable-5` the earlier wording was measured on, so the two wordings are not directly compared.

## The second Domain skill, `health-literacy`

`health-literacy`, writing member-facing copy a person can act on, is admitted on the same test. Prong (a) was checked on 5 member-facing samples drafted without the sources in view: a denied-claim notice, an EOB "amount you may owe" block, a prior-authorization message, a deductible-reset email, and an eligibility-lookup error. All 5 failed at least 2 substantive criteria and 4 failed at least 4; 4 read above grade 8, 2 near grade 14. The failures were the domain's own: insurer terms undefined in every sample, the action line passive in 4, the next step buried or absent in 3, and money shown as a prose formula rather than a figure.

Prong (c) was measured by 5 fresh-context probes against the final wording, none containing "health literacy", "plain language", or "readability". An eligibility-lookup 404 rewritten for the member portal, a deductible-reset email template, and a denial notice rendering "You owe $0.00" fired. An internal CSR admin-dashboard error carrying payer vocabulary declined the skill and quoted its anti-trigger back, and a claims-parser rename stayed quiet. That control matters most: an over-fire on internal text with the right vocabulary is the failure `phi-safe-code`'s NPI probe hit.

Its sources follow one rule: quote what is public domain, cite by name what is not. The cms.gov *Guidelines for effective writing* page is quoted for sentence and paragraph length, active voice, the language-to-avoid list, one term per concept, and scanning behavior. The plainlanguage.gov *Use simple words and phrases* list is cited by name, not vendored: it has no stable URL since the site redirects to digital.gov, and the general federal substitutions are not a gap in the model's default output. The payer vocabulary that is a gap is carried inline. The CMS *Toolkit for Making Written Material Clear and Effective* is cited by name in one lineage line for its money rules and for grade-level scores as ranges rather than a sole gate, and is quoted nowhere, because a contractor wrote it and its public-domain status is unverified.

A Domain skill defines its own field's terms in its body, not in `DOMAIN.md`. `health-literacy` defines *member-facing copy*, *the next move*, and *required language* in its opening, and needed no new glossary row.

## Consequences

- The suite is no longer purely repo-agnostic in subject matter; it stays repo-agnostic in mechanism, and the first lines of `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` say so.
- Two guards bind every Domain skill body: no org policy number in a `src/` body, and no `references/` file that is a regulation digest (that is a pack, which the test rejects). `phi-safe-code` holds both: its regulatory citations are `§164.514(b)(2)` for Safe Harbor, the stricter categories its § Boundary hands to a convention skill (42 CFR Part 2, CMIA, MHMDA), and the X12 acknowledgments its § Malformed input names (TA1, 999, 277CA). `health-literacy`'s three `references/` files (quoting policy, template branches, translation) are method, not a regulation digest.
- Live use was counted on 2026-08-26 on both machines, with the links present on both, and was zero for `phi-safe-code`, `health-literacy` and `accessible-ui`: no session in the window met member data, member copy, or a widget. The trigger claims rest on the probes above. How to count a real load, and the `StructuredOutput` echo that inflates a naive grep, are in [ADR-0051](0051-usage-evidence-mining-round.md).

Revisit when: a miss or over-fire on either description is reported, which re-runs the probes rather than re-wording the description; or a `claude-fable-5` run of the NPI probe is wanted to compare the two anti-trigger wordings directly.
