# Skills are judged by the Team-fit test; the retire-on-zero window binds stubs only

The 2026-08-21 round tied retirement to fire counts: [ADR-0051](0051-usage-evidence-mining-round.md) scheduled a retire check on `validate-behavior` and a flip-to-user-invoked check on `adoption-verdict`, [ADR-0056](0056-first-off-path-skill-change-quiz.md) put every Off-path skill on a retire-on-zero window, and [ADR-0058](0058-grill-me-absorbs-grill-and-record.md) put the `grill-and-record` stub on one. In the 2026-08-27 grill Nick decided that a count is not the criterion. The skills serve a product engineering team, and teammates' sessions are on machines nobody measures, so a zero on this machine or the work machine is evidence about load, never a verdict about use.

A skill is now admitted and kept by the **Team-fit test** (`DOMAIN.md`): its deciding record names a concrete moment in a product engineering team's work and the role who hits it, and it is retired only when an existing skill or global rule already serves that moment. Fire counts are evidence for the case. The call is the team lead's, stated in the record. `audit-skills` becomes the single home of the check, once the fold under *Deferred* lands: its Keep verdict will state the moment and the role, its Overlap lens is already the covered-by half, and the stub window will run under a Usage line that skill does not yet carry. Until then the check is the stocktake's existing Retire verdict, and no line in `audit-skills` names Team-fit. The retire-on-zero window survives for Deprecation stubs alone, because a stub serves one person's muscle memory and that person's typing history is the whole population.

Three alternatives were weighed. Keeping the window as written was rejected because its population is unmeasured for every real skill. Dropping the window entirely was rejected because a stub's typist is known, so its count is sound. An admission-only or retirement-only test was rejected because the first lets four skills serve one moment and the second never admits anything new. This amends ADR-0051, ADR-0056 and ADR-0058: their count-driven checks no longer run, and each record carries a pointer here.

## Consequences

- ADR-0051's one still-open check is canceled: the `validate-behavior` retire check, which its 2026-08-26 window kept open for a second zero. `validate-behavior` stays (the moment: an engineer proving a change works in the running app, not in tests). The `adoption-verdict` flip check was already closed on 2026-08-26 by [ADR-0032](0032-adoption-verdict-standalone-behavior.md) — one observed model-reached load settled the axis and no further check was scheduled — so this record does not cancel it; it restates the Team-fit case for the axis it holds (the moment arrives as a question in prose, often from a teammate who does not know the suite exists). The third of ADR-0051's three checks, the `grill-and-record` stub, is a Deprecation stub and closed on its own count, which stays the stub's test.
- The 2026-08-27 round admits `onboard-me` on this test ([ADR-0064](0064-onboard-me-read-only-kt-session.md)), and plans `rebuild-me` and `offboard-me` for the same round under their own records; each record names its moment and role. Neither of the latter two exists in `src/` or has a record at the time this is written.
- The clause-level retire hedges readers attach to folds ("retire on a window with no bare `UNVERIFIED:`") are dropped; a clause is kept or cut by the pruning test at review.
