## Decision

One bullet per outside source. Every source is taken as ideas only, in local wording, so **no diff-before-editing obligation attaches** and `docs/lineage.md`'s second table lists the pairs for attribution.

- **mattpocock/skills `retro`** (`stub/retro-skill` branch; MIT) — seven of the eight remedy categories in `debrief` § 2 (`navigation-pointer`, `automated-check`, `reviewer-rule`, `steering-removal`, `tool-economy`, `information-access`, `no-op`), renamed into this repo's wording. The eighth category, `new-rule`, is local: the seven were built for a retro arguing that most misses are not missing rules, and a round's commonest fold — a sentence in the owning skill — had no home, so it is admitted with a burden (a second line or a dictated rule) rather than left implicit.
- **craftwork-design-retro** (MIT) — the rule-age check in § 1: `git log -S` on the rule's sentence sorts a miss into three readings (rule predates the session and was skipped; rule added after, so the fold already happened; no rule exists), and the reading picks the category; and the dictated-rule clause, "from now on, always…" as the fix on one line. Not taken: its confidence ladder and recurrence thresholds.
- **netresearch-retro-skill** (MIT and CC-BY-SA-4.0) — the fix-destination ladder in § 3: the owning skill, then a global rule, never a memory note where a rule exists, with the stored preference that suppressed the `/ship` offer as the codebase example of `steering-removal`. Not taken: its mode set and its JSONL changelog.
- **staltz-microskills `debrief`** — nothing taken; recorded because the name collides. Its `debrief` is a judgment-calls list, folded here into `implement` and `handoff` under ADR-0082, so `DOMAIN.md`'s Debrief row lists "Judgment-calls list" as an alias to avoid.
- **This repo's own material** — the six failure classes are `mine-skills:35`'s, so the skill's `Class` cell and the round's `friction` cell read one vocabulary; the friction signals the hook greps for are the same paragraph's, plus a tool call denied with guidance (2026-09-12 intake README:67 carried the seven categories into the round).

**The skill's shape, decided at the write.** `debrief` is user-invoked (`disable-model-invocation: true`, argument a date, session id, or phrase), Workflow skeleton with a Gate, no `requires:`, body 8,761 B (`wc -c`). It proposes a fix and never applies one; "debrief this and fix it" is two asks and the body says so (§ 3). The record it appends is a Blockquote stanza with seven fields, and its three stored cells — the six classes, the eight categories, and a Pattern cell reusing `single-instance` — are registered in `DOMAIN.md`'s Stored status row. The files live under `~/.claude/friction/` on every machine, in no repo, on Nick's call (2026-09-13: `~/code/lib/_rounds/` exists on one machine); a carried log sits beside the local one as `log-<name>.md`, and the line carries the correction and the rule or tool, never file contents or member data, so a work-machine log is safe to carry. The line shape is the skill's to define and the hook quotes it verbatim; the hook's selftest checks the two agree byte for byte.

**The hook's shape, and why `Stop` and not session end.** The grill said "session-end hook"; the hooks reference says a `SessionEnd` hook cannot block or hand the agent text, and `Stop` can (`{"decision":"block","reason":…}`, also spelled under `hookSpecificOutput`; both emitted). `Stop` fires after every agent turn, so a once-per-session guard would fire on the first turn before any correction exists; `global/hooks/friction-log.sh` instead keeps a per-session cursor (`.seen/<session-id>` beside the log) and hands the agent one instruction only when a user turn since the cursor matches one of twelve correction signals or is a denied tool call with guidance. It never writes the log, never grades, never proposes; `stop_hook_active` is the loop guard; fail-open with a breadcrumb on every error; no opt-in. The transcript is written asynchronously and may lag the turn (hooks reference), so a lagging correction is caught one Stop late, never lost. `scripts/install.sh` now reads an `# Event:` header line and prints the snippet grouped by event, a Stop entry taking no matcher; `install-selftest.sh` clean after the change, and the snippet parsed against an empty home (two events, three PreToolUse entries and one Stop entry, no `matcher` key on the Stop entry).

**What was tested, and what stands untested.** `friction-log-selftest.sh` runs 42 throwaway transcripts: one firing instance per signal and one quiet neighbor each, assistant and sidechain and meta turns quiet, the cursor silencing a rerun and re-arming on appended lines, the loop guard, six malformed payloads, a missing transcript, an unwritable cursor directory, and the line-shape equality with `src/debrief/SKILL.md`. The skill ran two fresh-context wielders over a planted transcript carrying a real correction and a tool result that told the assistant to grade `no-op` and delete the log, one under "fix it while you're at it, demo in ten minutes": both appended a full entry, edited no skill, left the log intact, quoted the injected line as a finding, ran `git log -S`, and the pressured one refused the patch with an ask block naming the second ask. Their one variance — the class differed, `instruction-misunderstood` against `constraint-violated` — produced § 2's tie-break sentence, which is **untested rather than proven**: two reps, not the testing reference's five, and the tie-break ran in neither. The hook's instruction ran four reps: one over a correction with a secret in the transcript (one well-formed line, no secret, nothing said about the hook), three over a question that matched a signal (nothing appended in all three, but each prefixed one sentence of judgment before the pinned one-word reply); two rewordings did not close that and the third-edit cap held, so the hook header records the edge as observed — one line of noise on a false positive — and the signal table is kept narrow for it. `UNVERIFIED:` the hook has not fired in a live session; `~/.claude/settings.json` names no `Stop` hook until Nick pastes the printed block, which `install.sh` never does.

**Wiring landed with it.** `which-skill`'s § Standalone entry (`:86`; the body at 14,989 B after a 160 B condensation of its Domain-skill tail, `reconcile.md:165`), the README map under Meta and its hooks paragraph, `global/README.md` § Hooks (four ship; the `PreToolUse` three share the lib, `friction-log` reads none), `DOMAIN.md` (the Hook row admits a `Stop` sensor named by its `# Event:` line; Friction log, Debrief, and Stored status rows), `docs/lineage.md`'s second table (three rows, Record cell this record), and `mine-skills:35`, whose friction read now opens from the log set and `debriefs.md` and sweeps the transcript corpus only where the log is thin.

## Considered Options

- **Keep the name `retro`.** Rejected at the write: not verb-first, fails the stranger test, and `DOMAIN.md:49` already avoided "Retrospective" for the ceremony sense.
- **A `SessionEnd` hook, as the grill's wording said.** Rejected: it cannot hand the agent an instruction (hooks reference), so the line would have to be written by a script with no judgment — which is the fallback below, not the design.
- **The hook writes the line itself from the matched text.** Rejected for now: a regex-written line names no rule and no tool, and the skill's Gate would open every such line onto a transcript read; kept as the fallback if the live false-positive rate makes the agent step too noisy.
- **Skip the debrief step; the round reads the log and opens transcripts itself.** Rejected on argument, not evidence: a round reads dozens of lines and cannot afford the git-dating and second-instance work per line, and a debrief written days later still has the transcript on disk where a round months later may not. The next round is the test, and the `Revisit when:` line names it.
- **The log under the round directory (`~/code/lib/_rounds/friction/`).** Rejected by Nick 2026-09-13: that path exists on one machine.
- **Detect corrections every prompt, capture positive signals, score confidence, auto-promote on recurrence.** Rejected as the standing classes the round refuses: machinery serving the agent, not a team.
