# Diverging as a standalone behavior, reversing the fold-into-grilling stance

The 2026-07-03 sweep rejected a sibling brainstorming skill and folded its two salvageable moves into `grilling`, on the grounds that divergence lacked a bindable discipline of its own. The 2026-07-19 oaustegard sweep reverses that for a sharper candidate: `diverging` (ported from oaustegard's generative-thinking) ships as a standalone model-invoked behavior because it now has what the earlier candidate lacked — a discipline that binds (one move committed per invocation, the fire test, framings-not-ideas as the output contract) and a model-reachable trigger (fixation signals — repeated iterations converging on one idea — rather than stakes). Folding it into `grilling` was rejected because the two motions are contradictory in-loop: grilling evaluates every branch as it goes, while diverging's core rule is generation before evaluation — one skill body cannot hold both defaults without one weakening the other. `grilling` stays the convergent complement: diverging generates candidate frames, grilling stress-tests the chosen one.
