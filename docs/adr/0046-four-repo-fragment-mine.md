# Mine gsd-core, BMAD-METHOD, speckit-agent-skills, and agent-stuff as fragments only

The 2026-08-09 round mined 4 newly cloned repos for portable fragments: open-gsd/gsd-core (`next` at dc9b299b — its default branch; its `main` is dormant), bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD (ae7aface), dceoy/speckit-agent-skills (c04cbb7), and mitsuhiko/agent-stuff (d265b8e). Three sit in standing wholesale-reject classes — gsd-core is an SDD orchestration product, BMAD a persona framework, speckit the Spec Kit pipeline — and gsd-core's catalog listing was rejected unread in the 2026-07-19 discovery-index closure. We mined them anyway: reject classes ban wholesale ports, not idea mining. The first real read of gsd-core then reversed the unread call at the fragment level — its separately-authored test-and-verification research notes supplied the test-quality cluster, the round's largest. None of the 4 repos becomes a lineage upstream under [ADR-0034](0034-branch-mining-lineage-or-dormant-main.md); future sweeps read their default-branch tips only (for gsd-core that is `next`, never its dormant `main`).

## Consequences

The pre-mortem named dilution as this round's failure mode — 38 mined fragments (counted per digest entry; several land as more than one rule) is the largest single-round widening this repo has taken. The build therefore folds each fragment into existing rules rather than appending sections, and treats lint's size caps as a hard stop: a fragment that cannot land without pushing its host past the cap comes back as a named cut, not a squeeze (the newly adopted size-cap Goodhart guard makes raising a cap on approach itself a violation). No fragment hit the cap this round — the largest touched host closed at 161 of 200 lines. Reversal is the costly direction: the fold-ins carry no per-fragment markers, so unwinding the round means recovering every fragment from this record and the diff — which is why the cluster list above enumerates its hosts exhaustively.
