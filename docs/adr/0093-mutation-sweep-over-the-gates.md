# The guard-coverage mutation sweep: a hand-run script that mutates each gate one line at a time and names every site its selftest stays green over

## Decision

- **Two files under `scripts/`, hand-run, never a gate.** `mutation-sweep.sh` is the driver: it derives the roster the way `post-merge` and `lint-skills.sh` do (`scripts/*.sh` minus selftests, libraries and `setup-hooks.sh`; `scripts/git-hooks/*` with a shebang; `global/hooks/*.sh` with `# Install note:`) and adds what those leave out because they are not gates themselves — a `.py` beside a `.sh` takes that script's selftest, and a `*-lib.sh` or `*-lib.py` takes the selftests of every script in its directory that sources it, one library hop deep, so `hook-lib.py` is graded by the three hooks that source `hook-lib.sh` and `lint-lib.sh` by both linters. `selftest-lib.sh` is never a target: mutating the harness tests the tests. For each mutant it copies the working tree without `.git`, writes the mutant over the file in the copy, checks it still parses (`bash -n`, `ast.parse`), and runs each paired selftest there in its own process group under a timeout. `mutation-sweep.py` is the generator: three operators, one edit to one line each — `drop-alt` removes one alternative from a `|`-joined group in a quoted pattern or a bash `case` arm, `flip-test` inverts one comparison on a line that tests something (`-eq`/`-ne`, `-z`/`-n`, `=`/`!=` inside `[ ]`, `==`/`!=`, `is`/`is not`, `||`/`&&`), `drop-line` removes one reporting line (a `say_fail`, `hook_allow`, `crumb`, `emit`, a non-zero `exit` or `return`). A shell `|` or `||` inside a `"$(...)"` is not an alternative, a flip never lands inside a quoted string, and ids are `<op>:<line>:<n>` so a capped sample is the same set every run (first N by SHA-1 of the id).
- **Five verdicts, and only one is a finding.** CAUGHT: some paired selftest exits 1. SILENT: every paired selftest exits 0 or 2 — as far as it ran, it saw nothing — and this is the finding; one SILENT site fails the sweep (exit 1). INVALID: the mutant does not parse; counted, never run. BROKE: a selftest exits 4 over it; noticed by a crash, not a fixture, so listed and not counted as caught. HUNG: a selftest outran the timeout; listed, not counted either way. A target whose selftest is not green over the unmutated copy is UNGRADED and the run ends 2. A SILENT site is read by a person before it is fixed: the sweep cannot tell a fixture the selftest lacks from an equivalent mutant (an edit with no observable effect, such as an alternative in a roster nothing reads), and the first run produced both kinds.
- **Cost bounds the default, not the design.** A full pass costs one selftest run per mutant, `lint-skills-selftest.sh` takes about 70 s and `review-receipt-selftest.sh` about 45 s, and the big linters yield several hundred mutants each (`lint-skills.sh` 736 at this commit, `security.sh` 377; `mutation-sweep.py list <file> | wc -l`). So the default is `--max 10` per target, `--path` narrows to one script for a deeper pass, `--all` lifts the cap, `--jobs N` runs mutants in parallel on their own copies, and `--list` prints the roster and the sample without running. `sweep-corpus`'s gate loop does not run it; its selftest, `mutation-sweep-selftest.sh`, is the only thing `post-merge` and `pre-commit` pick up, and it grades the sweep on a fixture tree with one planted hole (about 12 s).
- **What the first run found, and what it taught.** The first full pass (`--max 10 --jobs 4`, 18 targets in the roster, 2026-09-13) graded 157 mutants over 17 targets before it was stopped: CAUGHT 123, INVALID 7, SILENT 27 (`awk` over the run's lines, `~/code/lib/_rounds/2026-09-12/mutation-sweep-run1.txt`, copied there from `$TMPDIR` so it outlives a reboot). It was stopped at the seventeenth target because one mutant, `hook-lib.py:104` (`if c == "#"` flipped to `!=` in the tokenizer's comment scan), sent the scanner into an endless loop, and the timeout of that draft — `perl -e 'alarm shift; exec @ARGV'` — did not end it: bash defers a signal until its foreground child exits, so the alarm fired at 126 s and the process sat for 34 minutes until the loop was killed by hand. The driver now forks the selftest into its own process group and kills the group at the alarm; the selftest plants a grandchild that must be dead afterwards. The regrade of the three targets that draft had not finished (`hook-lib.py`, and the sweep's own two files) took 595 s and graded that mutant HUNG. Of the 27 plus `hook-lib.py`'s 5 SILENT sites, 13 were in this session's own new code and were closed here: 11 in the sweep (its selftest lacked rows for a missing generator, a non-numeric `--max`, the generator's usage exits, a parenthesised regex group, and one instance per operator-table alternative; three alternatives in the structural and test rosters were dead and are gone from the tables) and 2 in the `friction-log.sh` wrapper (no python3 on `PATH`, no `friction-log.py` beside it). The final regrade of the sweep's two files is CAUGHT 18, HUNG 2, SILENT 0 (the two HUNG are a `--path` guard flipped so the selftest sweeps everything and outruns the 60 s floor, and the default-timeout arithmetic the selftest header names as unobservable). The other 19 stand unread in `## Deferred`, each a fixture the selftest lacks or an equivalent mutant. The condition that unparks `D2.8` — a first run finding at least one silent site — is met; the ledger row says so.

## Considered Options

- **Hand-written mutation tables in each selftest, as `security-selftest.sh` carries.** Kept, not replaced: a table row is the check that lands with a rule, and it grades the mutation its author feared. The sweep is the audit over the rows nobody wrote, and it found 32 sites the tables did not cover on its first sample of 10 per target.
- **A gate on `pre-commit` or `post-merge`.** Rejected: a full pass is hours at `--all` and tens of minutes at the default, and a gate that slow is disabled. The selftest is the gate; the sweep is scheduled by a person, and `sweep-corpus` can name it when a round wants it.
- **Random sampling.** Rejected for the deterministic hash order: a capped run that changes its sample every day cannot be re-run to check a fix, and `--id` would name a mutant nobody can find again.
- **Stopping at the first SILENT site per target.** Rejected: the first run's value was the shape of the holes across targets, and the cost of finishing a sample of 10 is the cost of 10 selftest runs.
- **Exec-with-alarm for the timeout.** Tried, and rejected by the first run: it does not end a selftest whose child loops.
