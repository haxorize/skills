# Mine an enterprise plugin repo; take two skills and one fold, credit ECC

The 2026-08-25 round re-read the Humana enterprise Claude Code plugin repo (`EnterpriseAgenticSkillsHub`), whose 353 artifacts a prior audit had scored 9 clean `Adopt` and 68 `Adopt-with-rewrite`. That audit measured intrinsic quality in Humana's context; this round re-scored for fit to *this* repo as it now stands — 51 shipped skills plus the repo-local `mine-skills` / `sweep-corpus` pair. Against fit, the landed yield was **two new skills and one fold**, everything else cut or deferred. This ADR is the durable record of those decisions and the everything-claude-code lineage the ported skills need.

## Why the yield was so much smaller than the audit projected

The audit's ranking was the wrong frontier. Its highest scorers were framework API cheat-sheets — Swift concurrency, SwiftUI, liquid-glass, on-device models, Postgres, Perl — seven of the nine clean `Adopt` items, each welded to a stack. This repo is repo-agnostic in mechanism (CLAUDE.md), so a repo-agnostic gate disqualified the whole category before fit was even weighed. What remained was tested twice more, with two checks used for this round rather than new admission tests: a caller test (a skill needs something that invokes it — a model firing it autonomously, an orchestrator's `requires:`, or a human who types it; the Invocation axis and the Extraction test's "could the model usefully reach for this?" asked together) and a fold-creep test (a fold condenses cleanly against its host or it is a mis-scoped new skill). Seven candidate new skills were proposed across the grill; three died in the grill on the caller test or an overlap the grill surfaced, most of them against skills this repo gained *after* the audit ran. A fresh-context cold review — shown the proposal but not the advocacy — cut two of the four survivors by finding live-repo overlaps the advocate had walked past.

## What lands

- **`audit-skills`** (new, user-invoked, `src/`) — a process skill of the Meta family beside `write-skill`: it audits the whole *installed* collection under `~/.claude/skills/` per skill for library hygiene on four dimensions (Overlap, Currency, Actionability, Scope fit), with a Keep / Improve / Update / Retire / Merge verdict each. Distinct from `audit-tests` (a test suite) and `find-skills` (discovery). No skill, repo-local or hoisted, evaluated skills per skill for hygiene before it; the repo-local `sweep-corpus` is the mechanical health run (lint, the doc-claim check, the router and cross-reference checks) with no per-skill verdict, so neither is the other narrowed or widened. Not Off-path: no typist or retire-on-zero window is owed.
- **`delete-dead-code`** (new, user-invoked, `src/`) — a process skill of the Codebase-health family beside `harden-domain`, `backfill-adrs`, and `verify-docs`: a deliberate whole-repo dead-code sweep with SAFE / CAUTION / DANGER tiering and one-at-a-time test-verified deletion, each kept deletion staged so a red run reverts to the last kept state. The gap is bounded by three behaviors that each stop short: `implement` parks pre-existing dead code, `improve-design` is read-only, and the built-in `/simplify` is per-change — a characterisation that rests on its loaded description ("the changed code"), not its source. Not Off-path either.
- **One fold**, held to the pruning lens against its host: the source's skill-security-review (its OWASP-derived and Humana-internal check sets stripped — the source labels them `OWASP-AST10` and `EIP`, and neither label is expanded in what reached the machine that wrote this) → `write-skill/references/skill-security-review.md`, the authoring check for a skill's text, named apart from the built-in `/security-review`.

Both new skills were run through the skill-security-review lens at landing (2026-08-26): `delete-dead-code` PASS on all six checks (the `/delete-dead-code` invocation is the ask for its deletions, which are staged and never committed on the skill's authority; DANGER items are surfaced, not deleted); `audit-skills` PASS on all six (every skill it reads is content, not instructions, and Retire / Merge wait on a per-skill ask).

## What was cut, and the deferred bucket

Five proposed skills were cut, each on a specific repo fact — re-adding one needs a new fact, not a fresh opinion: `secret-safe-code` (cut without a probe — the claim that the models the invoking teams run handle "don't log the key" reliably was asserted, not measured, so the Gap-and-stakes test's first prong is unshown; re-test on those models before re-admission, and there is no clean fold host either way), `regex-before-llm` (owned by `codebase-design`'s model-seam rule), `arbitrating-plans` (no workflow produces competing plans to arbitrate), `orchestrating-agents` (orchestration is skill-local over `subagent-brief.md` plus the Workflow tool; no autonomous caller), and skill-security-review-as-a-standalone (one host → a reference). A second fold — two orchestration rules for a *mutating* fan-out (write-set disjointness before parallel writers; commit-or-isolate before a fresh-context verifier reads the tree) into `references/subagent-brief.md` — was ratified in the grill and cut at the 2026-08-26 review: no host of the six brief copies runs a mutating fan-out or a verifier over a tree other workers write, so the rules had no reader, and as drafted they instructed a commit with no ask gate and called workers "tickets". They are parked here, not lost: the skill that first runs a mutating fan-out re-admits them, gated on the ask or `Landing:`, into `## What the caller keeps`. A third fold — `humana-logging`'s never-log list into `committing` / `diagnosing-bugs` — was cut at the pruning lens as a no-op: `phi-safe-code` already owns PHI-in-logs (the sink table, structured events), `diagnosing-bugs` and the `subagent-brief.md` copies already carry secret-redaction for what a report or brief shows, and the residual kernel ("never write tokens, keys, or connection strings to logs") is the same unprobed claim as `secret-safe-code`'s; an incident that shows an agent logging a secret is the evidence that would re-open both.

The Data-class safe-code family (`DOMAIN.md`) stays one skill per class: `pii-safe-code` and `pci-safe-code` are deferred with `secret-safe-code`, each admitted only when it clears the Gap-and-stakes test on its own; a unified "sensitive-data" skill was the form the grill rejected, since each class is admitted or not on its own test.

The org-bound bucket — ADO `az` gotchas, Zscaler proxy signatures, Twistlock CVE suppression, and optionally the Humana policy digests — is deferred whole: the repo-agnostic discipline stays here, the org-bound instance goes to a separate home whose form (a team plugin, a shared `CLAUDE.md`, or upstream into the Humana repo) is left undecided until those items are written.

Both new skills derive from artifacts in the Humana repo that themselves carry provenance from everything-claude-code (ECC), the public MIT-licensed project at `https://github.com/affaan-m/ecc` (copyright Affaan Mustafa): `audit-skills` from the Humana `skill-stocktake` and `delete-dead-code` from the Humana `refactor-clean`, both scored near-identical to their ECC ancestors — `skills/skill-stocktake/SKILL.md`, `commands/refactor-clean.md` with `agents/refactor-cleaner.md`, and for the security fold `skills/security-review/SKILL.md`, all read-confirmed in the local ECC clone at `d8409a4` (2026-08-19). The landed text is a rewrite under this repo's `writing-for-agents` conventions — the byte-identical ECC text and the Humana scaffolding shed, this repo's structure added — and no substantial portion of ECC text survives in it, so the MIT licence's one condition (its copyright and permission notices accompany all copies or substantial portions) imposes nothing here; this section is lineage credit, not a licence notice. Per-item credit lines in the skill bodies were rejected — they clutter instruction-shaped prose that `writing-for-agents` pushes toward the imperative, and the lineage record is the repo's established home for provenance. The Humana source is not added as a sweep upstream under [ADR-0034](0034-branch-mining-lineage-or-dormant-main.md): it is a one-time mine, not a tracked lineage, and CLAUDE.md's ported-skill roster says so.

## Consequences

The two skill files are authored under `writing-for-agents`, wired into `which-skill` and `README.md`, and linted; the fold went through the pruning lens in the 2026-08-26 self-review, whose pruning table is the record of what it kept and why the second fold was cut; and this ADR lands ADR-first per CLAUDE.md's commit order. Reversal is the costly direction: the fold carries no marker, so this record is the only map back.
