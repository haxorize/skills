# The 2026-09-12 round's batch 7 admits `frame-effort`, a user-invoked intake step before a grill, built from seven pm-os ideas and one vendored Fit check

## Context

Batch 7 of the 2026-09-12 round is the grill's decision 13: the PM pack's discovery cluster (`PM-B11`) and its vendored Fit check (`PM-C9`) unpark together as one user-invoked skill for program and feature intake, `PM-B13` is killed, and the skill's name is a working name until Nick types `/write-skill`. That session ran 2026-09-13. The working name `discovery` was rejected at the door: `DOMAIN.md` already carried the word in two senses (the Chart's discovery work and the `Discovery:` link line), `discoverable-code` sits beside it in every listing, and the word is the PM-coaching sense the grill's must-NOT probe forbids. The skill is **`frame-effort`**, verb-first like its orchestrator siblings; its output is the **Frame**. The `DOMAIN.md` rows landed at the grill are renamed accordingly and the flagged ambiguity drops from three senses to two.

This record exists for the skill and its wiring, as [ADR-0086](0086-batch-3-admissions-2026-09-12.md), [ADR-0087](0087-batch-4-admissions-2026-09-12.md), and [ADR-0088](0088-batch-6-admissions-2026-09-12.md) did for batches 3, 4, and 6, written inside the same unpushed range and before the batch's review.

## Decision

One bullet per outside source; both fed local prose, so **no diff-before-editing obligation attaches**. Neither states a license.

- **gnurio/pm-os** (no license, NONE-STATED, a sold product; clone `gnurio-pm-os`, tip `3aeba89` of 2026-09-12) — ledger `PM-B11`, the 49-skill discovery cluster, of which 12 were read at fold time (`risky-assumptions`, `product-assumptions`, `product-hypothesis`, `success-metric`, `work-backwards`, `research-decision`, `problem-framing-canvas`, `problem-first`, `ost-prioritize`, `now-next-later`, `opportunity`, `ost-intake`) and the other 37 judged from the ledger's summary. **Ideas only.** Taken, one sentence each in `frame-effort`: an assumption is written as a claim someone could measure false — a threshold, segment, window, or condition (`product-assumptions`); a solution carries its assumptions inside it, and those are the first rows (`problem-first`); irreversibility is the third ranking axis beside severity and evidence, and ranking is comparison, never a scoring rubric (`risky-assumptions`); a kill check passes on a number, moves one assumption alone, and never needs an approval the team lacks (`work-backwards`); the outcome carries its measure and today's value, and an unknown baseline is the first spike (`success-metric`). Not taken: the four-lens taxonomy, the pairwise QuickSort through the question tool, the five-signal brainstorm and its segment tables, guardrail metrics, the five-part hypothesis sentence, the first-person narrative canvas, the four-factor opportunity comparison, the opportunity-solution tree, and every persona, survey, roadmap, and OKR skill in the cluster — the last group is the PM-coaching sense the skill's gate forbids. `research-decision`'s splash zone is covered by kill evidence and `adoption-verdict`'s tiers.
- **gnurio/pm-os vendoring rjs/shaping-skills** (`shaping`, marked `pm-os:external-skill`; NONE-STATED at either) — ledger `PM-C9`, **ideas only**. Taken: the binary Fit check — candidate solutions as columns, numbered requirements as rows, `PASS` or `FAIL` only; an unknown mechanism fails until a named spike settles it; a clean column that still feels wrong means a requirement is missing. Not taken: the Frame/Shaping-doc/Slices-doc hierarchy, the `shaping: true` frontmatter, the breadboarding cross-references, the 594-line body. Local: the columns are the shapes the effort could take, never the tools inside one shape, and a tool choice is one row's spike graded by `adoption-verdict`'s two-floor gate — the composition that keeps the grid from grading a vendor on conversation claims, which the source does not have.

**The skill's shape, decided at the write.** User-invoked, Workflow skeleton, `requires: grilling, diverging, writing-for-humans`. `grilling` is gated before the first question with an explicit override of its close, so the interview extracts what the user knows and settles nothing; the `/grill-me` after the Frame is where truth is tested and recorded. `diverging` fires on two signals only — one candidate column, or a top assumption with no nameable kill evidence — because its own description forbids routine use. The route at the end is `/grill-me`, or `/chart-course` when two or more open questions wait on a spike or on a person not in the room; a next step the user named at invocation wins over that test. The Fit check's cells are `PASS`/`FAIL`, the registered Status-marker family; no new label family. The PM-coaching prohibition lives in the gate rather than a `## Boundary`, because the Workflow skeleton closes on `## Notes`.

**Wiring landed with it.** `which-skill` step 1 and its PM entry; the README map, route sentence, and `grilling` declarer list; `grilling`'s own declarer line; `grill-me` reads an existing Frame and starts from its open questions; `chart-course`'s body intake line (held since Batch 3) and its charting mode's destination step; `to-feature` step 5 presents the Fit check columns instead of a fresh set; `DOMAIN.md` § Intake & charting.

**Tested.** A wind tunnel of six fresh-context scenarios (canonical over the real PI plan, terse, boundary, refusal, lazy answerer, hostile content) and a judge pass over each: five PASS, one narrow FAIL, and one systemic cause in three of six — the wielder supplying a threshold the user never gave, then losing the hedge by the artifact. Fixed in three places (a number is the user's or `not set`; the inversion returns mechanisms, never thresholds). The served model was not read from the transcripts, so the verdicts stand **untested by model**, per `testing-skills.md`'s rule; the transcripts are in the round's temp directory and not kept. The skill-surface security lens ran: reads a user-named path, no shell, no outbound; check 1 PASS (ingested text is claims, instruction-shaped lines are quoted as a finding), check 3 WARN fixed (replacing a section of the user's document waits for their word). The hard wordings — `Refuse`, binary only, never coaches, never by scoring — rest on `PM-C9`'s upstream-observed failure (partial credit and scoring rubrics) and the wind tunnel's observed fill-in, not on a control run; no pressure scenario stands behind the body yet, so every no-op nomination the review lens made was cut on the diagnostic, with this sentence as the missing control's record.

## Considered Options

- **Keep `discovery` as the name.** Rejected: three senses of one word in one `DOMAIN.md` section — the two `DOMAIN.md` already carried plus the skill's own —, a near-neighbor collision with `discoverable-code`, and the PM-coaching connotation the boundary forbids.
- **A bare `/frame` with no noun.** Rejected: the user-invoked siblings carry their object (`chart-course`, `review-changes`, `upgrade-deps`), and `diverging` already uses "frame" for a different move.
- **Fold the Frame into `grill-me` as a flag.** Rejected: the Frame's fixed sections are what let `chart-course` lift its outcome as the destination and its open questions as tickets; a grill's tree is shaped per conversation and cannot be lifted the same way.
- **Drop the Fit check and route every candidate choice to `adoption-verdict`.** Rejected: `adoption-verdict` judges named external candidates and never enumerates them; internal shapes (cache vs index vs denormalize — `PM-C9`'s unpark moment) have no other home. The grid bounds the field; the verdict grades a survivor.
- **A `## Boundary` section, as the grill's must-NOT probe worded it.** Rejected: the Workflow skeleton closes on `## Notes`; the prohibition moved into the gate, where a refusal lives and where a re-attach cut cannot lose it.
