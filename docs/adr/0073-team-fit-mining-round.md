# Mine 53 new sources under the Team-fit test; fold as clauses and climb the failing rules to mechanisms

The 2026-08-27 round read 53 previously unseen sources and 16 saved URLs, delta-mined the 123 repos that had moved since 2026-08-21, and re-heard 70 rejections and parks. It ran as 18 sessions on a 13-number spine (`grep -cE '^[0-9]+[a-z]?\. \*\*' batch-plan.md` → 18; the numbers are `0 1 2 2a 2b 3 3a 4 5 6 6a 7 8 9 9a 10 11 12`) and landed 48 commits over 233 files, +6,315/-759, with 10 new records — ADR-0063 through ADR-0072, this being the eleventh and the last batch's whole deliverable. The suite went from **52 skills to 57** (`git ls-tree -d --name-only e1b4423 src/ | wc -l` → 52; the same at `HEAD` → 57). The predecessor round's record, [ADR-0051](0051-usage-evidence-mining-round.md), is the shape this one copies; the substance is different in two ways, and both are the round's thesis.

The first: **a fire count stopped being the criterion**. [ADR-0063](0063-team-fit-test-replaces-retire-on-zero-window.md) owns that decision and this record only names it. The second: the round's own reading produced almost no prose to fold. Every one of the 15 readers returned **0 ADOPT** but one, and the survivors were clauses inside rules that already existed. What the round actually spent itself on was the other end of the escalation ladder — the rules that had already failed as prose climbed to lint checks, git hooks, and scripts. Batches 2b, 9 and 9a carried most of that weight: 9a is machinery alone, and 2b and 9 each pair one rule change with the check that makes it hold. The round's centre of gravity is there, not in the 80 ADAPT rows.

This record clears the ADR gate on all three criteria. **Hard to reverse:** the folds carry no markers, so this record and `~/code/lib/_rounds/2026-08-27/batch-plan.md` are the only map back to what changed and why — reversing a clause means finding it first. **Surprising without context:** a reader who finds `lint-skills.sh` grown by several hundred lines in one week, or three new user-invoked skills nobody has typed, will ask what happened. **A real trade-off:** the alternative was the round the evidence would have justified on its own terms — fold the 80 ADAPT rows and stop, spending nothing on machinery for rules that were already written down. A rational team picks that; it is cheaper, and it does not restructure two gate scripts. It lost because this round's own corrections were prose rules failing again in the presence of their own text, which is the case [ADR-0053](0053-global-rules-layer.md)'s escalation ladder exists to answer.

## Method changes

Four things were new and are kept.

**Every reader was judged against the suite as it stands, not as it stood.** The 2026-08-21 round's readers worked against a suite missing nine of today's hosts. This round re-heard all 70 host-absent rejections and parks under the Team-fit test in `rehearing-2026-08-27.md`, and its counts are that file's own: 70 examined, **STANDS 45, RE-HEAR-ADAPT 19, RE-HEAR-NEW-SKILL 6**, with the awk that measured them printed above the table. Nineteen of the round's folds enter the batches from that re-hearing rather than from a source.

**Every prior PARK was marked met or unmet before the ledger closed.** `park-sweep-2026-08-21.md` walks all 51 PARK rows of the previous round: **MET 10 · UNMET 20 · UNVERIFIABLE-HERE 15 · REHEARD-THIS-ROUND 5 · SUPERSEDED 1**, summing to 51, each with its evidence line. The 15 UNVERIFIABLE-HERE rows are the honest cost of a two-machine setup — a park whose trigger is a work-machine event cannot be swept from this machine, and saying so beats guessing. This step is now `mine-skills` §5.

**A separate reading pass for the Domain hosts, security-scanned first.** The three Domain skills had never been mined against; batch 4 read 31 repos shortlisted for them. The round's own security scan covered 3 of those 31, so the other 28 were scanned before a reader opened them. The verdicts are over all **31**, which is the scope `security-scan-domaincut.txt`'s own header states — RISK 5 · REVIEW 5 · PASS 21 — every flag domain prose rather than a payload.

**A measurement before a mechanism.** Batch 9 carried a mechanisms backlog of ideas that had accumulated as "this should be a lint check". Four were measured first, and two of them died on the measurement — recorded below under *Mechanisms*. The practice this establishes: an escalation from prose to machinery names the failure the machinery would have caught, and runs a control that shows the prose missing it. Unlike the other three method changes, this one nearly **landed in no skill body** — the Team-fit lens and the park sweep are `mine-skills` steps and the Domain-cut security scan is that skill's existing rule, while this would have lived here and nowhere else. It is now enforced rather than remembered: [`predictability.md`](../../src/writing-for-agents/references/predictability.md)'s form-to-failure table already carried the rung above prose ("a hook or lint that sees the violation"), and that rung gains the clause that the mechanism is built only after a control run shows the prose actually missing the failure. It is one clause inside an existing rule, not a new rung — `writing-for-agents` is a thin host, and the measurement is a condition on an escalation the table already prescribes rather than a separate instruction. It carries no pointer back to this record, because a skill body may not cite an ADR (`lint-skills.sh`'s `check_adr_citation`); this paragraph is the only place the two are tied together.

## Five new skills, and the Team-fit judgments

Five skills entered the suite, and the Team-fit test is why. Four of them were PARK rows in **this** round's own ledger — `~/code/lib/_rounds/2026-08-27/ledger.md` lines 107, 162, 165 and 168, each marked `PARK (new-skill candidate)` for want of a second consumer: `describe-product` (N1a.53), `onboard-me` (N1b.22), `offboard-me` (N1b.25) and `rebuild-me` (N1b.28). The fifth, `evaluation-ledger`, entered by a different door: it has no ledger row at all, and [ADR-0071](0071-evaluation-ledger-multi-week-evaluation-in-repo.md) admits it on reachability from the re-hearing row A41.OP1, not on an unparked park. Under the retire-on-zero regime that park could never resolve: a skill nobody has typed cannot produce the fire count that would admit it. The Team-fit test asks instead whether the record names a concrete moment in a product engineering team's work and the role who hits it.
