# Reading a scan result

Open this only when a scanner or CI accessibility result is in hand and is being turned into ledger lines. The body's rule stands: a scan is evidence on its rules and the bytes it ran on, never a verdict.

A scan detects a minority of the criteria, and for those only some failures — the rest of the ledger needs another run or the Human-required state. Read each part of the result on its own terms:

- Its **pass** is a pass on its rules, and on the bytes it ran on — an edit after it is unscanned.
- Its **failure** is a finding to confirm against the criterion it cites.
- A rule it **does not have** is not a criterion that passed.
- Its **"incomplete" or "needs review" bucket** is a Flagged or Human-required line, never dropped.
- Its **impact labels and scores** are triage, never the ledger's state.
