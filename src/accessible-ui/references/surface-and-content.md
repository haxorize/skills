# Surface and content

Open this only when the change alters what the rendered surface shows or how it reflows — color, contrast, or a forced-colors palette; motion or timing; target size or pointer behavior; heading, list, table, or language structure; layout, type, spacing, or the viewport meta — never on a change that only alters a control's semantics, keyboard path, or focus. The body's building rules stand; these are the five it hands off.

- **Meaning is not carried by color, position, or shape alone.** An error state has an icon or text beside the red; a required field has more than an asterisk color; a chart has a text alternative that states what the chart shows (the chart contract is patterns.md's). Forced colors repaints the author's palette, so a state carried only by a background fill flattens away and an icon drawn as a CSS background vanishes — the cue is a border, a glyph, or an inline `<svg>` taking `currentColor`.
- **Motion and timing respect the person.** Gate all non-essential motion on the person's reduced-motion setting, and let nothing auto-advance, flash, or time out without a way to stop it — the mechanics and criteria: [motion-and-timing.md](motion-and-timing.md), opened when the change touches motion or timing.
- **Targets and pointers.** A target is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (2.5.8), or a 24-pixel circle centered on it overlaps no other target; a drag has a single-pointer alternative, and a down-event never completes an action on its own (up-event activation is patterns.md's). A destructive or binding submission is reversible, confirmed, or reviewable first (3.3.4, patterns.md's).
- **Text is text.** Headings are heading elements in order; lists are list elements; a table of data is a `<table>` with header cells; text in images is avoided; the shell carries a skip link to the main content. The page declares its language (3.1.1), and a block in another one (3.1.2) — a payer's language-assistance notice inside an English letter — declares its own `lang`, and `dir` where the script runs the other way, so it is spoken in that language's voice; what it says is `health-literacy`'s.
- **Text scales.** Text survives zoom, reflow, and user text spacing — nothing clips, nothing needs two-dimensional scrolling, and no viewport meta forbids zoom; the thresholds: [scaling-and-reflow.md](scaling-and-reflow.md), opened when the change alters layout, type, spacing, or the viewport meta.
